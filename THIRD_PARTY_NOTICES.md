# Third-Party Notices

No third-party software, copied code, bundled assets, datasets, or model weights
have been incorporated into the repository at this time.

Package lockfiles and a future SPDX SBOM will be the machine-readable inventory.
This file is the human-readable attribution index and does not replace complete
license texts, upstream `NOTICE` files, or other required compliance materials.
